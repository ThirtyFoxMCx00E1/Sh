space_atlas.png is baked from the mod's own space textures:
- Earth: NASA Blue Marble / Black Marble (see ../moonfall/CREDITS.txt)
- Moon: NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit (LRO)
- Planets, Sun, Saturn ring: Solar System Scope (INOVE), CC BY 4.0, based on NASA data
- Milky Way: NASA/JPL-Caltech/ESO/R. Hurt (eso1339g), CC BY 4.0; panorama: ESO
Generic glow sprites (soft_glow, lens_star, ring, streak) are procedurally generated.
